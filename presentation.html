🛡️ 3-Layer Anti-Fraud & Compliance Architecture (Zero Fake Invoices) RBI & GST Compliant
1. GST Suvidha Provider (GSP) Live API handshake with GSTN server validates supplier & buyer GSTIN, GSTR-1 filings, and e-Way bills.
2. E-Invoice QR / IRN Validator Cryptographic SHA-256 hash verification of the Invoice Reference Number directly blocks Photoshopped fake bills.
3. RBI Account Aggregator (AA) Direct bank-to-bank financial statement pulls verify historical buyer payment tracks with zero manual tampering.

Demo: Start to Finish (5 Steps)

1
Personalized Login / Sign Up: User signs up with custom username (e.g. Keerthivasan) or Google Sign-In. Personalized greeting and profile display immediately.
2

5
Lender Approval & Disbursement: Switch to Lender Portal $\to$ View request $\to$ 1-Click Approve. Instant status update: "Disbursement Initiated (48h)".
